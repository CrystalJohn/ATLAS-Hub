### Source/claim controls

- Japan is the travel destination; Narita Airport is in Narita City, Chiba, rather than central Tokyo. The official company profile and airport access page are cited above. These scenes say “Tokyo-trip” and “fictional practice terminal” and do not suggest a terminal map is real.
- Every station, stop, line, exit, train time, price, menu and shop policy presented in a passage is fictional and self-contained. The learner must be taught that exact passage before its item.
- No statement is generalized to all Japanese people, shops, restaurants or transit systems.
- DE-### IDs in the scenario records are ideas from CONTENT-01’s 55-item scenario library, not published lessons. They provide product traceability, not learner exposure.
- Original ATLAS dialogue is separated from source facts. A source website did not author or endorse it.
