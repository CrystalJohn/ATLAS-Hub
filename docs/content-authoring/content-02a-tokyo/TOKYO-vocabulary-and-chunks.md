## Reuse and evidence notes

Senses and chunks have stable IDs within this DRAFT pack. Reuse in Singapore, London or workplace contexts requires local source/fact and register review; intent reuse does not make venue facts portable. Recognition, recall and controlled composition remain distinct evidence labels. No vocabulary quiz alone supports mastery.
