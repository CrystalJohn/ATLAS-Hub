# ATLAS learning content direction

ATLAS serves everyday Vietnamese English learners. Practical comprehension and communication lead; world knowledge supplies an engaging setting.

Design the normal path as: explore → understand useful English → interact → learn a little about the world → continue.

- Keep beginner missions short and focused on 3–5 practical words or expressions. Reuse them in observations, ranger exchanges and choices.
- Let the player choose the reading target (A1/A2/B1/B2). Character level never assigns language proficiency.
- Explain unfamiliar ideas in ordinary language before asking the player to use them. Specialist knowledge, calculations and opening source material are not prerequisites.
- Use natural questions, comparisons, requests and follow-up questions. Keep concise Vietnamese assistance optional.
- Keep authored factual claims linked to credible references internally. Put technical reports, citations and scientific detail in optional Learn More disclosures.
- Preserve clear labels for fictional scenes, simulated readings and unconfirmed reports. Simplification must not turn a possibility into a proven cause.
- Keep English evidence separate from general-knowledge mastery. Reading a dialogue is not listening or speaking evidence.
- Preserve server-authoritative scoring, XP, unlocks and reward idempotency. No client-side completion shortcuts.
