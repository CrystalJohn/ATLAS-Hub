## Limits and next expansion

This is a private playable pilot with authored deterministic dialogue, not an educator-certified course. A2–B1 is the priority; shorter/longer reading variants are available, with some reusable phrases shared between levels. No listening/speaking assessment or supplied pronunciation audio is claimed; Cambridge links provide a reliable pronunciation reference. No runtime AI translations, dialogue generation or voice evaluation were added.

Small countries/territories without a boundary at the selected map scale remain searchable. Boundaries are generalized Natural Earth data, not an authoritative statement about territorial disputes. The practice route is explicitly fictional.

Desktop uses environment + bounded side panel; longer optional sources can scroll inside the panel. Mobile uses artwork + compact activity + optional object/vocabulary/source controls and can still scroll on small screens. Real touch hardware, screen-reader speech output and a broad browser/device matrix remain follow-up QA.

Recommended next country: Singapore, after user review of Tokyo and educator review of its English/Vietnamese copy. Scope a similarly short arrival/directions/food/cultural encounter pack, research official sources and create licensed/original scenes before marking it PLAYABLE. Kyoto remains unimplemented until Tokyo is accepted.
