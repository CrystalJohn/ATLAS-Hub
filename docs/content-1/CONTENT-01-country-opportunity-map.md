Japan: [JNTO Tokyo](https://www.japan.travel/en/destinations/kanto/tokyo/), [GO TOKYO transport](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/plan/getting-around/index.html). Canada: [Destination Canada](https://info.destinationcanada.com/). Australia: [Tourism Australia marketing/reference guide](https://www.tourism.australia.com/content/dam/digital/corporate/documents/wwta-guide-march-2024.pdf). US: use the owning airport, city transit, NPS or municipal page per mission; no single generic source is sufficient.

## First three packs

1. **Japan — Tokyo continuation:** lowest integration risk because the current pack already has Tokyo, travel encounters, bilingual copy and source IDs. Expand from the taught airport/directions/food intents only after authoring new source-backed encounters.
2. **Singapore — arrival and MRT:** compact, multilingual, high practical value and strong transfer to existing sign/route/order mechanics.
3. **United Kingdom — London transport and café/workplace English:** high reuse of directions, platform, request, correction and everyday interaction patterns; keep cultural claims local and non-universal.
