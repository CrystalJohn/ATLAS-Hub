## License rule used by ATLAS

- **GREEN — reusable:** exact page states a license/permission that covers the intended use. CC BY allows adaptation and commercial use with attribution; CC BY-SA adds same-license obligations; CC BY-NC blocks commercial use; CC BY-ND blocks distributed adaptations. [Creative Commons licenses](https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/use-remix/cc-licenses/) and [CC FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/) were inspected directly.
- **YELLOW — facts/reference only:** use the source to verify facts, procedures, terminology or learning principles; write original text and visuals. Do not copy passages, questions, audio or screenshots.
- **RED — unusable:** rights unclear, prohibited, or incompatible with the intended distribution.
- **REVIEW REQUIRED:** potentially usable but asset/page-specific rights, personality/privacy, trademark, ShareAlike or commercial interpretation needs human/legal review.
